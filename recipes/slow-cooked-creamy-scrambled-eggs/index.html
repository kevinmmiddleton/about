Slow-Cooked Creamy Scrambled Eggs

The Gordon Ramsay method, minus the crème fraîche because I'm not buying crème fraîche for eggs.

Serves
2
Prep
2 min
Cook
10 min
Total
12 min
Calories
330

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs (about 300g)
  • 30g unsalted butter
  • 30ml whole milk
  • 3g salt

Instructions

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, and salt.
  2. Melt butter in a nonstick pan over very low heat.
  3. Add eggs and stir constantly with a silicone spatula.
  4. Cook slowly 8-10 minutes until soft and custardy.
  5. Remove just before fully set; residual heat finishes them.

Notes

Pull them off the heat when they still look slightly underdone. They'll finish on the plate.
