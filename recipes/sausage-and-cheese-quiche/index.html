Sausage and Cheese Quiche

Serves 6-8 slices · Prep 15 min · Cook 40 min · Total 1 hr 10 min

The ratio here is forgiving. More cheese, different sausage, whatever. It'll work.

Ingredients

For the Crust

  • 1 pie crust, blind baked

For the Custard

  • 200g eggs
  • 200g milk
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 3g garlic powder
  • 3g onion powder
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Red pepper flakes to taste
  • Sage to taste
  • Parsley to taste

For the Filling

  • 150-200g sausage
  • 100-120g cheese, shredded

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F / 175°C.
  2. Brown the sausage in a skillet, breaking into crumbles. Drain excess fat.
  3. Whisk together eggs, milk, melted butter, and all spices.
  4. Layer sausage and cheese into the blind-baked crust.
  5. Pour custard over the filling.
  6. Bake 35-45 minutes until edges are set and center has a slight jiggle.
  7. Rest 10-15 minutes before slicing.
Notes: Blind bake the crust or the bottom will be soggy. I mean it.
