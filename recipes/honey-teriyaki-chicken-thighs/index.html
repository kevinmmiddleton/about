Honey-Teriyaki Chicken Thighs

Serves 3-4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min · Total 1 hr · About 360 cal per serving

Sticky, slightly sweet, dead simple. Good over rice, obviously.

Ingredients

  • 680g boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 90g soy sauce
  • 90g dry white wine
  • 45g honey
  • 10g garlic, minced
  • 2g red pepper flakes
  • 10g neutral oil

Instructions

  1. Whisk soy sauce, wine, honey, garlic, and pepper flakes.
  2. Marinate chicken 30-60 minutes.
  3. Heat pan with oil over medium-high.
  4. Sear thighs 4-5 minutes per side.
  5. Add remaining marinade and simmer until thick and glossy.
  6. Reduce until sauce coats the chicken.
Notes: Thighs are a forgiving cut, which is why they work well here. Let the sauce reduce longer than you think. It should coat the chicken, not pool around it.
