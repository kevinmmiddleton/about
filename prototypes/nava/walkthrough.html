What each release should move

ReleaseWhat shipsWhat should moveProgram goal

Watch every release: Where applicants stop and don't come back. Corrections per application also falls when people give up, so we need to track abandonment to ensure we've fixed the problem instead of moving it.
Where I'd start

The first four weeks

1
Sit with case workers. Not interviews. Watch the queue on a normal Tuesday.
2
Pull the denial and correction data. The reason list should come from what actually fails here, not from what I guessed.
3
Measure where applicants stop. Nobody has that number today, and without it we can't tell whether R4 fixed the problem or just moved it.
4
Confirm one policy question. Can a corrected document be accepted without redoing the whole review?
On employers: they're not in this six months, but how fast they respond affects how long a decision takes. If everything else improves and that number doesn't, employers are the bottleneck and they go first next.
