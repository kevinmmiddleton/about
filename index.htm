About Me

I make complex work feel simple.

I build platforms, tools, and AI workflows that connect teams and systems.

I've been an engineer, a consultant, and now a product leader, leading teams, owning revenue lines, and building platforms at enterprise scale.

In my experience, teams usually know what's wrong. They just need someone willing to name it and make it safe to act on. That's usually where I start.

I work across people, process, and product. I'm hands-on at every level: running meetings, cleaning up details, digging into data, or building a quick prototype.

Most of my best work happens between teams that don't usually talk to each other. Engineering and support. Product and marketing. I broker the agreements, bring the context, and make sure nobody's working in the dark.
